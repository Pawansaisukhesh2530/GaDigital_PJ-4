Transformers

Inverter Duty Transformers

These transformers are designed to integrate seamlessly with inverter-based power systems. Their VPI windings provide excellent insulation against high-frequency harmonics, voltage surges, and thermal stress, ensuring steady performance in renewable and industrial power conversion setups.

They are widely used in solar, wind, UPS, and variable frequency drive (VFD) applications, where reliability, low loss, and harmonic mitigation are critical. Their air-cooled construction allows safe indoor installation without compromising performance.

Key Features & Benefits
  • Designed for inverter-based and VFD systems.
  • Handles non-linear and fluctuating loads efficiently.
  • Superior harmonic resistance and insulation quality.
  • No oil or fire hazard.
  • Compact and lightweight construction.
  • High dielectric strength for surge protection.
  • Efficient cooling through Air Natural (AN) system.
  • Long operational life under variable frequency loads.
  • Reduced electromagnetic interference (EMI).
  • Customizable voltage ratios for different inverter systems.
